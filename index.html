Research note

Every Bubble Builds Something

Caroline Roth · MS Finance, Fordham University · November 2026

The ten largest US companies now make up 40.6% of the S&P 500, far more than at the 2000 dot-com peak, and eight of them are AI companies. Unlike 2000, these leaders are priced close to their long-run average relative to expected earnings, and they already earn 36% of all S&P 500 profits. The risk for ordinary investors isn’t Cisco-style valuations; it’s how much of a ‘diversified’ index fund, or a default retirement fund, now depends on one theme.

                  Recovery time

                  Recovery time = months from the start of the scenario until the balance regains its starting value after its lowest point. With contributions, recovery means the balance is back above the total amount invested.

                  Limitations & next steps

                  • Valuation comparisons with 2000 use figures read from J.P. Morgan’s charts; company-level data would make them more precise.
                  • International stocks are modeled with US industry returns in the crash replays.
                  • Future extensions: an AI buildout test comparing hyperscaler capital spending with AI revenue (the modern version of the 1990s fiber-optic overbuild), and an AI-washing index measuring how often S&P 500 companies mention AI in their 10-K filings.
